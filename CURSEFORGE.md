# Gnomish Pachinko

### *A word from Tinkmaster Overspark, Chief Engineer of Tinker Town*

Well hello there! Tinkmaster Overspark, at your service. Pull up a crate and mind the sparks, because you're about to meet my finest invention: **the Gnomish Pachinko Machine!**

It fits right inside your game. Point with the mouse, click, and a polished steel ball goes rattling down through a board of pegs. Light up every **orange peg** and the board is yours. You get ten balls a level, so make them count!

And once you've got the hang of it, build boards of your own in my **level editor** and send them in. The best ones go into the machine for everybody, with your name on them!

---

## What's inside the machine

**400 levels across 40 chapters**, every one of them somewhere in good old Azeroth. Dun Morogh, Elwynn, the Barrens, Silithus and the rest, each with its own painted board. No portals to strange new worlds. I'm an engineer, not a magician!

**Four hosts, eight powers.** I don't run the whole show myself. Each chapter one of us minds the machine, and when you hit a **green peg** you get that host's power:

- **Me, Tinkmaster Overspark:** Multiball, and my Super Guide (it shows you where the ball will bounce).
- **High Tinker Mekkatorque:** Space Blast and Chain Lightning.
- **Razzle Sprysprocket:** the Pyramid (a stone pyramid that kicks your ball back up) and Free Ball Frenzy.
- **Bink:** the Fireball and the Spooky Ball.

**All sorts of puzzles:**
- **Eggs** to hatch, sitting in little brick cradles. Knock the cradle out and the egg falls, so catch it!
- **Gems** to knock loose and drop off the bottom.
- **Super Slides:** rails of bricks that carry your ball all the way round.
- **Balloons, bumpers, wheels, pendulums, keys and cages,** and a few moving parts I'm especially proud of.

**Fever!** Light the last orange and the bottom turns into my five G-N-O-M-E vacuum tubes. Fill every one of them for a whopping bonus.

**Duels with my brother.** Cogwhistle. Don't get me started. Clear his board and he'll challenge you to a duel: five balls each, turn and turn about. And somewhere along the way he and I finally settle things ourselves. Win that one for me and I'll show you my **Crazy Guide**.

**Bosses!** Rogue machines from my own workshop: the Tin Drake, the Bolt Golem, the Gyro Spider, the Mechano-Boar and the Cog Yeti. Each one fights back in its own way. Light orange pegs to zap them, or hit them square for double damage.

> **Fair warning:** the bosses are still a work in progress. They may change completely in a later version!

**Build your own levels!** My workshop is open to everyone. Press **Level editor** under the map (or type **/pachinko editor**) and lay out a board of your own: pegs, bricks, Super Slides, balloons, bumpers, keys and cages, eggs, gems and moving parts. Pick the goals, test-play it on the spot, and save as many as you like.

Proud of one? Press **Export code** and post the code in the **comments here on CurseForge**. I try out every one I'm sent, and the best go into the machine itself for everybody to play, with **your name on the level card** as its builder.

**Fully voiced.** Every one of us talks to you, and the bosses grumble and hiss and boom in voices all their own. There's music, a crowd that holds its breath on the last peg, and bottle rockets when you earn your stars.

## Plays and Golden Gears

You get **free plays every day.** Clearing a level costs you nothing; losing one spends a play. Every 24 hours, open the machine and press **Claim** for 5 more free plays (come by each day: a missed day isn't saved up). You can bank up to 50 free plays; at 50 the daily plays wait until you dip below.

Want more? That's what **Golden Gears** are for, the machine's own coin. **One gold buys one gear.** Walk up to any mailbox and press **Get Golden Gears**, and I'll fill the mail out for you.

> **Mind this:** I will not, and I *cannot*, press Send for you. The Blizzard... ahem, the *Gnomish* Regulators forbid it! That last button is yours to press.

Then spend your gears in the **Golden Gear Shop:**

| Item | Gears |
|---|---|
| 3 Suction Tubes | 1 |
| Extra Green Peg | 1 |
| 3 Rings of Fire | 2 |
| 3 Rainbow Balls | 3 |
| 5 plays | 10 |

Your progress, gears and special balls are sealed up tight in my vault, so no tinkering with the saved files!

## Getting started

- Type **/pachinko** to open the machine, or click the little minimap button.
- **/pachinko levels** opens the map. **/pachinko editor** opens the level editor. **/pachinko sound**, **/pachinko music** and **/pachinko voice** switch things on and off.
- Hold the **right mouse button** to zoom in and line up a tricky shot.

I'll walk you through the rest myself the first time round. Special balls, green pegs, the lot!
