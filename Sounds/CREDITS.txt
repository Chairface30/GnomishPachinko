Gnomish Pachinko - Sound Credits
================================

Every sound effect in this folder is generated with ElevenLabs' sound-effects
model by tools/gen_sfx.py (the prompts are in that file; tools/generated.json
records what was made and when). The combo scale, note1.ogg .. note16.ogg, is
one generated marimba note resampled to a C major scale over two octaves.

The announcer's lines in Voice/ are generated with ElevenLabs (Eleven v4) by
tools/gen_voice.py, in a voice of the pachinko's own.

Earlier versions used three Kenney "Casino Audio" clips and three Freesound
clips, all CC0; none remain.

This addon is distributed free of charge. Do not resell these sounds standalone.
